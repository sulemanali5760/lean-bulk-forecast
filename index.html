    How it works: your maintenance calories come from the Mifflin-St Jeor formula times your activity level. Every ticked meal adds its calories; anything above maintenance turns into weight at about 7,700 kcal per kg. A weigh-in resets the model to the real number. Days you don't log count as maintenance, so they add nothing. Scale weight swings up to 1 kg a day with water and food, so compare weekly, not daily.
